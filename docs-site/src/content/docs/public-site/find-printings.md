---
title: 'Find in Lists'
description: See every other copy of one card across all your lists, straight from the card you are looking at.
---

**Find in Lists** answers "where else do I have a version of this card?" for the card you're looking at. It searches every deck, collection, and wanted list on the site and shows each physical copy, grouped by list. Use it to compare the printing in a deck against the copies in your binders, or to spot that a collection card is already played in a deck or sitting in a trade binder.

Like [Find Cards](/public-site/find/), this is a public-site browsing feature only. It never writes anything.

## Opening the lookup

Two entry points, both for a single card:

- **From the card ⋯ menu**: hover a card, open its **⋯** menu, and choose **Find in Lists**. The ⋯ menu is available on every list page, including combined views and Find results. Outside edit mode it holds just this lookup; in edit mode it sits alongside the edit actions.
- **From the card detail modal**: click a card to open its modal, then press **Find in Lists**.

Both open the same dialog, titled **Other Copies**.

## What it shows

Every copy of the card across every list, matched by **front-face name**, the same rule the Find page uses. A lookup on `Steam Vents` includes a line written as `Steam Vents // Steam Vents`, and vice versa.

Copies are **not merged**. A `3x` deck line appears as three cards, and each collection entry stands alone with its own printing, finish, and condition. Results are grouped by list, with the list you're viewing first, titled **Current List — _\<list name\>_**.

Two display modes, toggled at the top of the dialog (the choice is remembered for the session):

- **Binder**: a card-art grid. Foil copies shimmer as in the normal list views.
- **List**: compact rows showing each copy's set, collector number, finish, condition, language (uppercase, when the entry records one), and price, with the same hover art preview and foil sheen as list-view pages.

## Jumping to a copy

Click any copy to go to it. The site opens that copy's list, scrolls to the card, and briefly highlights it. If the copy is in the list you're already viewing, the dialog closes and jumps to the card in place.
